1. What we collect

What you enter

Your account (email or sign-in provider), country and currency, salary day, budgets, expenses and income, obligations and debts, pantry items, shopping lists, pharmacy items and dose times, appointments, subscriptions, documents you choose to scan, family chat messages, family tasks, and what you write or say to Zad's assistant.

What you allow through permissions

Each permission is optional and asked for when you first use the feature.

  • Notification access. Zad reads the notifications that bank and payment messages arrive in, so it can record a purchase for you. Bank messages usually arrive inside your messaging app's notification, so Zad reads that app's notifications too and keeps only the ones that look like a bank or payment message. A notification Zad cannot classify on the phone is sent to our server to be classified, and is kept only if it is a transaction. Zad never reads bank logins, card numbers, PINs, CVV codes or one-time passwords, and ignores OTP messages.
  • Location. One approximate fix when you ask for nearby shops and local prices. Only if you turn on store-arrival reminders, location is also used in the background, and only for the shops you saved. You can turn it off in Zad or in Android settings at any time.
  • Camera and photos. For scanning receipts, pantry items, medicines and documents you choose to photograph.
  • Microphone. Only while you hold the voice button to speak to Zad.
  • Notifications, exact alarms, battery optimisation exemption. For reminders (doses, appointments, bills) and to keep bank-message reading working in the background.

Collected automatically

Device model, Android version, app version, crash reports and diagnostics, approximate feature usage, and your IP address. Crash reports are sent without personal data or screenshots. On the free plan, Google AdMob collects the advertising ID and device data to show and measure ads (see section 5).

2. TikTok data

Connecting TikTok is optional. Zad uses TikTok's official APIs only (TikTok Login Kit, Content Posting API and, where granted, the Research API). It never scrapes TikTok or uses unofficial libraries, and it never sees your TikTok password.

2.1 If you connect your TikTok account

DataWhy Zad uses it

Zad posts only after you preview the content and tap to share, with the privacy level and settings you choose. It never posts automatically or in the background, never reads your private messages, followers or watch history, and never uses your TikTok data for advertising.

2.2 Public TikTok content and research data

To suggest recipes and local offers, Zad may read public TikTok videos and their descriptions, found through search or, where granted, TikTok's Research API. This data contains nothing from your Zad account. It is used only to find and link to public recipe and deal videos, with credit and a link back to the creator, and for aggregate analysis. It is not used to profile or identify individual TikTok users, is never sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers, and is refreshed or deleted as TikTok's terms require, including when a video is deleted or made private.

2.3 Disconnecting and deleting TikTok data

You can disconnect TikTok in Zad's settings or from "Manage app permissions" in your TikTok settings. When you disconnect, Zad deletes your TikTok tokens and the TikTok profile data it stored within 30 days, and usually at once. Deleting your Zad account also deletes all TikTok data linked to it. Content you already posted remains on TikTok, where you control it under TikTok's Privacy Policy.

3. How we use data

To run the features you use; to record and categorise your spending; to remind you; to answer you through the assistant; to keep your family's shared data in sync; to share content to TikTok when you ask; to prevent abuse and enforce usage limits; to fix crashes; to process purchases; and, on the free plan, to show ads. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to train our own AI models.

5. Ads

The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob: rewarded ads you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads at natural pauses. Paid plans show no ads. Child accounts and kids mode show no ads at all. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

6. Family and children

Zad is for adults (18 and over). An adult can create a family and invite members. A parent may add a child member, who signs in to their own account inside the family, or turn on PIN-protected kids mode on their own phone. Child members and kids mode get no ads and no advertising ID, cannot connect TikTok, and see a reduced app (tasks, savings challenges, family chat). A parent can view and delete a child's data.

School zones are optional. A parent can be told when a child leaves a place the parent set, during hours the parent set, only after the child agrees on their own phone. The child's phone shows a permanent notice while it is on, and either side can stop it. Zad sends only "entered" and "left" events and does not store the child's route. Zad does not offer location tracking of any adult.

7. How we protect data

  • All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Every table on our server is protected by row-level access rules, so only you, and family members you share with, can read your data.
  • Secret keys and third-party tokens, including TikTok tokens, stay on the server and never ship in the app.
  • Access to production systems is limited to the people who run Zad.
  • If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

8. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. TikTok tokens are kept only while TikTok is connected. Public TikTok content is kept only as long as it is public and TikTok's terms allow. Backups are overwritten within 30 days. Data the law requires us to keep is kept only as long as required.

9. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: Zad → My account → Delete account.
  • By email: write to astralabs.supp@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" from the email address on your account.

Your account is closed at once and stops receiving messages. You have 14 days to restore it by signing in again. After 14 days your account and all its data, including any TikTok tokens and TikTok profile data, are erased. A minimal record that a deletion happened (no amounts and no text) is kept for security. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law or Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law), you have the right to access, correct, export and delete your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can do most of this in the app: see and correct your data, export it, withdraw any permission in Android settings, disconnect TikTok or Telegram, and delete your account. For anything else, email astralabs.supp@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union. Where required, we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses offered by those providers.

12. Changes
