5.1 Your TikTok account and TikTok's terms

  • Connecting a TikTok account to Zad is optional. You connect it through TikTok's own authorization screen (TikTok Login Kit), where TikTok shows you exactly which permissions ("scopes") Zad is asking for. Zad never sees or stores your TikTok password.

5.2 Content Posting

  • Where Zad offers to share content to TikTok (for example a recipe video or a cooking summary you made in Zad), it does so only after you tap to share. Zad never posts automatically, on a schedule, or in the background.
  • Before anything is sent, Zad shows you a preview of the content and lets you edit the caption and choose the privacy level and interaction settings TikTok makes available for your account (for example who can view, comment, duet or stitch). Zad does not pick a privacy setting for you.
  • If you choose to send the content to your TikTok inbox as a draft, you finish and publish it yourself in the TikTok app.
  • You are responsible for the content you post. It must be yours or properly licensed, and must follow TikTok's rules, including clear disclosure of any branded or promotional content. Zad does not add watermarks, logos or promotional text to your content without telling you first.

5.3 Research and public content

  • To suggest recipes and local offers, Zad may read public TikTok videos and their descriptions. Where Zad has been granted access to TikTok's Research API, that data is used only as TikTok's research terms allow: to find and link to public recipe and deal videos, and for aggregate analysis. It is not used to profile, identify or contact individual TikTok users, and it is never sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Zad shows TikTok content with a link back to the original video and its creator, and keeps such data current and deletes it when TikTok requires it, including when a video is deleted or made private.

5.4 Disconnecting TikTok

You can disconnect TikTok at any time from the place in Zad's settings where you connected it, or remove Zad from the "Manage app permissions" page in your TikTok settings. When you disconnect, Zad deletes your TikTok access tokens and the TikTok profile information it stored. Videos you already published remain on TikTok under your control.
